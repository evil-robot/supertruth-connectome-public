# Substantiation summary: model comparisons in the connectome study

Prepared 21 September 2026 for counsel review. Every figure below is read from logged files in the private working repository evil-robot/supertruth-connectome (llm_arm/llm_runs/<run id>/). Nothing is estimated.

## What the comparison measures

Agreement with SuperTruth's own Data Trust Index (DTI) engine on synthetic records, not correctness about the records and not a ranking of vendors. Every public surface says so: the paper (Sections 3.8, 4.1, 5), the research page, the press release, the blog post, the company article. Each surface carries the nominative-use footer: model names identify what was tested; each name is the property of its owner; none of the companies is affiliated with SuperTruth and none has reviewed or endorsed the work.

## Records

300 synthetic records, tier-stratified (60 per tier) from the held-out test split of a 20,000-record synthetic corpus generated by schema with seed 20260920. Zero real patient data. The same 300 record ids for every model and both rounds (id digest recorded in each run).

## Calls

Every model was called through its vendor's own public API from SuperTruth's own paid account on 20 September 2026, with one identical prompt (system prompt sha256 5e7f4295...), three repeats per record, 900 calls per run. Round g: the published DTI paper plus an instruction block, no examples. Round g2 (post-hoc, disclosed as such): the same plus 100 engine-scored example records from the training split, zero overlap with the 300 scored or the 4,001 test records.

## Prices

Cost per record = tokens actually billed (from each API response's usage fields) times the vendor's published list price on 20 September 2026, recorded in prices.json in each run directory with the source:

- OpenAI: developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing and the GPT-5 model page ($1.25 input, $0.125 cached input, $10 output per million tokens).
- xAI: docs.x.ai/docs/models and api.x.ai/v1/language-models/grok-4.3 ($1.25 input, $0.20 cached, $2.50 output). xAI's own billed total (cost_in_usd_ticks) reconciles to our figure to the cent.
- Google: ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/pricing ($0.50 input, $0.05 cached, $3.00 output), plus $0.28 explicit cache storage on the g2 run, included.
- Anthropic: $5 input, $0.50 cache read, $6.25 cache write, $25 output per million tokens, as recorded from Anthropic's model reference at the time of the run. Counsel may wish to confirm against anthropic.com/pricing on the day of publication.

## Consistency claims

"Same answer every time": the fly returned bitwise-identical output on repeat (deterministic forward pass, recorded per run). Models: share of records with identical trust tier across the three repeats, round g: Gemini 84.0%, Grok 72.3%, GPT-5 67.3%, Claude 95.3%; byte-identical output on all three repeats, round g2: Claude 3.0%, GPT-5 0.3%, Grok 1.0%, Gemini 28.3%. The company article's "repeated its own answer word for word less than a third of the time" is the Gemini g2 exact-match figure (28.3%), the best of the four.

## Disclosures carried on every surface

1. Agreement with SuperTruth's own engine, not correctness; ranks no vendor.
2. The examples round is post-hoc (added after the pre-registered protocol; amendment row 17, dated).
3. The fly trained on 13,999 records; the models saw at most 100 examples; fine-tuning was not run (paper Section 5).
4. Claude Opus 5 g2: 11 of 900 calls hit the 16,000-token output cap and were kept as unparsed; every record retained at least one parsed repeat.
5. Two of five pre-registered seeds complete; provisional; updated at the same DOI.
6. Model versions served may differ from the vendors' newest; a different version, setting or prompt can change the answers.
7. Synthetic records only; zero PHI. DTI and BII do not diagnose, treat or recommend for any patient.
